⚙ Private DNS

Tap the button to request Samsung’s Private DNS screen directly. If your browser blocks the request, use Open Android Settings as a fallback.

Samsung phones

Settings → Connections → More connection settings → Private DNS

Other Android phones

Open Settings and search for “Private DNS”, or look under Network & internet.
